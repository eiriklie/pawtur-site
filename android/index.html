Åpne i Google Play

Gruppen brukes bare til testingen. Du får ingen e-post fra den, og de andre i gruppen ser ikke adressen din. Vil du slutte å teste, kan du melde deg ut på siden for testere i steg 2 og forlate gruppen. Les mer i personvernerklæringen.

Test Pawtur on Android

Pawtur by Eirik Lie

The Android app is being tested on Google Play before it is released to everyone. Join and test it, ideally together with the rest of your pack. You need an Android phone with Android 10 or later and Google Play.

1
Join the test group

Sign in with the Google account you use on Google Play, and tap "Join group".

Open the test group
2
Become a tester

Tap "Become a tester". It can take a few minutes after you joined the group before it works.

Become a tester
3
Install Pawtur

Get the app on Google Play, and sign in with Google. If someone in your family already uses Pawtur, also on iPhone, tap "I have an invite" and use their code. Otherwise, create a new pack.

Open in Google Play

Google requires the app to be tested by at least 12 people for 14 days before it can be published. Please keep the app, and use it on your walks during that time. Send bugs and feedback to support@pawtur.app.

The group is only used for the test. You get no email from it, and the others in the group do not see your address. To stop testing, leave the test on the tester page in step 2 and leave the group. Read more in the privacy policy.
